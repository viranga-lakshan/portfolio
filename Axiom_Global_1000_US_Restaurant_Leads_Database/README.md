1. **Younger, Tech-Native Founders (Ages 28–45):**
   - Millennials & Gen-Z operators who grew up on mobile apps, QR ordering, and digital payments.
   - They despise paying 15%–30% delivery app commissions and actively seek 0% commission on-premise technology.
2. **Accessible Direct Communication Channels:**
   - **Direct Business Email:** Direct founder & personal email addresses (e.g. `brandon@via313.com`, `tatsu@ramen-tatsuya.com`, `sven@coyo-taco.com`).

   - High-traffic venues: Craft breweries with big patios, Detroit & artisan pizza spots with constant lines, vibrant modern taquerias, ramen & bao bars, hot chicken & smash burger joints, weekend brunch hotspots.
   - Table capacities ranging from **35 to 90 tables**, where servers get bogged down taking repeat drink orders or splitting bills.
