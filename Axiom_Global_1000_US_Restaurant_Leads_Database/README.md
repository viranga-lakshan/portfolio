## 18 Required Fields Captured Per Record

1. `Lead ID`: Formatted unique ID (`AXM-USA-0001` to `AXM-USA-1000`).
2. `Restaurant Name`: Official registered business name.
3. `Owner / CEO Name`: Full verified name of the executive decision-maker.
4. `Job Title`: Owner, Founder, CEO, President, Managing Partner, Executive Chef & Owner.
5. `Restaurant Category`: Standardized cuisine categorization.
6. `City`: Primary operating city.
7. `State`: 2-letter US state postal code.
8. `Official Website`: Verified primary website domain.
9. `Business Email`: Verified public contact email (`info@`, `contact@`, `hello@`, or "Not publicly available").
10. `Direct Business Phone`: Standard formatted US telephone number `(XXX) XXX-XXXX`.
11. `WhatsApp Number`: Confirmed WhatsApp number or "Not publicly available".
12. `LinkedIn Profile`: Decision-maker verified LinkedIn profile/search link.
13. `Company LinkedIn`: Company official LinkedIn corporate page.
14. `Number of Locations`: Total verified operating venues (1 to 115).
15. `Source URL`: Evidence page (About Us, Press, James Beard, State Registry).
16. `Contact Source`: Verification source for contact coordinates.
17. `QR Ordering Suitability`: High, Medium, or Low rating.
18. `Lead Score`: Calculated 0–100 score based on Section 7 rubric.
19. `Priority`: "High Priority" (80–100), "Medium Priority" (60–79), "Low Priority" (<60).

## Lead Scoring Methodology (Section 7 Rubric)

Every prospect was evaluated against a strict 100-point rubric:
- **Dine-in table service:** 25 points
- **Multiple tables & regular customer orders:** 20 points
- **Independent ownership or accessible decision-maker:** 20 points
- **Multiple locations:** 15 points (Single location: 10 points)
- **Existing digital technology adoption:** 10 points
- **Publicly available business contact information:** 10 points (0 points if email is missing)

**Result:**
- **High Priority (80–100 Points):** 1,000 leads (100%) — All selected leads operate full table service with established dining rooms, accessible executive leadership, and active digital footprints.
