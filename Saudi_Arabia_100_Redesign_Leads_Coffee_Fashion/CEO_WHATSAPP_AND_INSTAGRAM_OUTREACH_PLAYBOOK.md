# Saudi Arabia CEO WhatsApp & Instagram DM Outreach Playbook
## How to Pitch Speculative Animated Redesigns to Saudi Founders

In Saudi Arabia, business is deeply relational and aesthetic-driven. Traditional cold emails often have low open rates among boutique brand founders. However, **WhatsApp and Instagram DMs have open rates exceeding 85%** when approached with respect, visual proof, and cultural fluency.

---

## 🎥 The Secret Weapon: The 20-Second Prototype Video

Never send a cold message asking: *"Do you need web development?"* The answer will always be *"No."*

Instead, send a **20-second video of their actual brand already redesigned** in 2026 interactive scroll style:
1. Load their logo and 2–3 of their real products into your animated template.
2. Record your screen scrolling smoothly down the page (showing parallax movement, smooth hover interactions, and mobile responsiveness).
3. Export as a crisp, short MP4 video.
4. Attach the video directly to your WhatsApp or Instagram message before writing your text!

## 💰 Commercial Pricing & Negotiation Strategy

When the CEO replies: *"How much does this cost?"* or *"How does this work with our Salla/Zid store?"*

### 1. Handling the Technical Integration (Salla / Zid / Shopify):
- **Option A (Headless Storefront):** Connect your animated React / Next.js / Webflow front-end directly to Salla / Zid / Shopify APIs so all inventory, orders, and payment gateways remain in their existing merchant account!
- **Option B (Hero Landing + Direct Checkout):** Use the animated site as their primary flagship landing page (`brand.com`) with instant "Buy Now" buttons that redirect directly into their native Salla/Shopify checkout.

### 2. Pricing Tiers for Saudi Arabia:
- **Standard Interactive Redesign:** \$2,500 – \$3,500 USD (~9,500 – 13,000 SAR)
  * Includes customized scroll parallax, 3D product view, mobile responsiveness, and payment hookup.
- **Full Deluxe Brand Experience:** \$4,500 – \$6,000 USD (~17,000 – 22,500 SAR)
  * Includes custom 3D modeling, interactive flavor/fabric quiz, bilingual Arabic/English RTL support, and launch handover.

## 📅 Daily Outreach Rhythm
- **Goal:** Reach out to **5 founders per day**.
- **Process:** 
  - Morning: Prepare 1 coffee prototype and 1 fashion prototype.
  - Afternoon: Record 20-second screen captures customized with brand logos.
  - Evening (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM KSA Time, when Saudi founders are most active on their phones): Send WhatsApp and Instagram DMs.
- With 100 targeted leads, a conservative 4%–6% closing rate yields **4 to 6 closed clients = \$12,000 to \$25,000 USD in revenue**.
