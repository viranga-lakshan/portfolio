QUESTION 1 Have you built a B2B ordering website with customer-specific pricing?

Yes, absolutely. We have architected and deployed multiple enterprise B2B purchasing portals featuring complex, multi-tiered contracted pricing matrices:

  • Role & Company-Based Pricing: Separate companies and branch locations are assigned custom price groups (e.g., Client A pays £42.00 for CNC Bushing Part #RP-104, while Client B pays £38.50 based on annual contract volume).
  • Confidentiality by Design: Unauthenticated public visitors see either list pricing, catalogue-only mode, or "Login for Agreed Pricing" prompts. Logged-in buyers only ever see their own negotiated rates.
  • Bulk Discount & Quantity Breaks: Tiered pricing rules (e.g., 1–5 units, 6–20 units, 21+ units) applied automatically at the shopping basket stage.

SECTION 07 · DEEP TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
System Design · Parts Catalogue, PO Checkout & PDF Drawings

Technical Architecture & B2B Ordering Workflows

How the Reynolds Precision Engineering B2B ordering portal functions behind the scenes to deliver a fast, frictionless ordering experience for plant maintenance engineers.

1. Multi-Company & Multi-Factory Accounts

Corporate clients (e.g. Kerry Group, Moy Park, Dale Farm) can have multiple buyer accounts under one parent entity, with separate delivery addresses for different factory sites across the UK and Ireland.

• Contracted pricing assigned to parent account auto-inherits to all sub-locations.
• Secure password management and optional two-factor authentication (2FA).
2. Engineering Catalogue & PDF Drawings

Each precision component product page features part numbers, material specifications, tolerances, and an instant "Download PDF Drawing" button.

• PDF engineering drawings protected behind authorized customer login.
• Live fuzzy search by part number, machine model, or component keyword.
3. Frictionless B2B Purchase Order (PO) Checkout Workflow
[Step 1: Selection] Customer selects Part #RP-204 → Views contracted rate (£45.00) & Available Stock (6 in stock) ↓ [Step 2: Made-to-Order Logic] If quantity exceeds 6, system prompts: "6 available immediately; 4 Made-to-Order (Lead time: 5-7 working days)" ↓ [Step 3: PO Checkout] Buyer adds to basket → Proceeds to checkout → Enters Company PO Number: [ PO-2026-8941 ] ↓ (No credit card or online payment required · B2B Credit Account Billing) [Step 4: Automated Dispatch] System triggers branded PDF Order Confirmation email to Customer & Reynolds Workshop Queue ↓ [Step 5: Repeat Ordering] Order stored in Customer Portal → 1-click "Re-Order This Part" button for regular replacements
4. Stock Status & Manufacturing Lead Time Engine
Component State Frontend Customer Display Backend Workshop Action
In Stock Off-the-Shelf IN STOCK Dispatch within 24 Hours. Generates pick-and-pack dispatch slip for off-the-shelf inventory.
Manufactured to Order MADE TO ORDER CNC Lead Time: 5–7 Working Days. Automatically creates a CNC work order ticket in the Reynolds admin queue.
Split Availability Partial stock dispatched immediately; remaining balance scheduled on CNC machine run. Generates split delivery notes with transparent tracking for the customer.
