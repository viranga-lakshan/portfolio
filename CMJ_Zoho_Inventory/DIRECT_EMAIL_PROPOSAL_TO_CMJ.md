### Core Areas of Alignment for CMJ:

1. **Standardized SKU Taxonomy Formula:** Replacing ambiguous descriptions with an intelligent, human-readable SKU structure like `[CAT]-[TYPE]-[MAT]-[DIM]-[HAND]-[FINISH]` (e.g., `DR-WD-SLD-3070-RH-RAW` or `HW-LCK-MRT-ENT-US26D`) for instant barcode scanning and error-free order picking.
2. **Composite Items & Product Combos (BOM):** Setting up auto-assembly for pre-hung door units and opening packages so selling a package automatically decrements slabs, frames, hinges, and locksets from inventory in real time.
3. **Safe Duplicate Resolution:** Running programmatic duplicate identification scripts to merge or archive duplicate items into single canonical master SKUs while preserving complete historical accounting and invoice records.
4. **Supplier Costs, Landed Costs & Tiered Markups:** Managing vendor cost sheets, freight allocations, and automated markup rules per customer tier (General Contractors, Commercial Subcontractors, Architects, Retail).
5. **Real-Time Zoho Books Synchronization:** Ensuring inventory assets, vendor bills, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) post automatically into Zoho Books with zero double-entry.
6. **Custom Deluge Automations & Alerts:** Building margin-floor guardrails (alerts if gross profit drops below target) and supplier cost-hike notifications.
