### How We Address CMJ's Scope of Work:

* **Organize Products & Variation Matrices:**  
  We structure your catalog into clean Item Groups (Wood Doors, Hollow Metal, Hinges, Locks, Closers, Accessories) with attributes for Dimensions, Handing (LH/RH/LHR/RHR), Core Types (Solid Core, Hollow Metal, Mineral), and Finishes (US26D, US10B, Black, Primed).

* **Standardized SKU Taxonomy Formula:**  
  We replace vague item descriptions with an intuitive, standardized SKU format such as `[CAT]-[TYPE]-[MAT]-[DIM]-[HAND]-[FINISH]` (e.g., `DR-WD-SLD-3070-RH-RAW` or `HW-LCK-MRT-ENT-US26D`). This enables instant barcode scanning, faster warehouse picking, and eliminates order errors.

* **Composite Items & Product Combos (BOM):**  
  We build Composite Items for pre-hung door units and commercial opening packages (Slab + Frame + 3x Hinges + Lockset + Closer + Threshold). When a composite package is sold, Zoho Inventory automatically deducts each component from available stock in real-time.

* **Catalog Deduplication (Zero Data Loss):**  
  We run programmatic duplicate detection scripts against your existing inventory to identify duplicate clusters, map them to a canonical master SKU, consolidate stock counts, and safely inactivate legacy duplicates while preserving historical accounting records.

* **Supplier Costs, Landed Costs & Tiered Markups:**  
  We configure vendor cost sheets, freight/landed cost allocations, and automated markup percentage rules per customer tier (General Contractors, Commercial Subcontractors, Architects, Retail).

* **Stock Tracking, Purchasing & Fulfillment:**  
  We configure warehouse bin locations, automated reorder points based on supplier lead times, 1-click Purchase Order generation, and full fulfillment workflows (Sales Order → Pick/Pack → Shipping Slip → Invoice).

* **Zoho Books Bi-Directional Financial Sync:**  
  We establish seamless chart-of-accounts mapping so inventory sales, vendor bills, landed costs, and COGS post automatically into Zoho Books in real-time without double entry.

* **Custom Deluge Automations:**  
  We write custom scripts, such as a **margin-floor guardrail** (flags quotes where gross margin falls below CMJ's threshold) and **supplier cost-hike alerts** (notifies staff when vendor price increases require selling price adjustments).
