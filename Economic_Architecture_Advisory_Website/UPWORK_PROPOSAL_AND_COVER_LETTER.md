### 4. Infrastructure, Mail Security & Deliverability (`econarc.com.sa`)
To ensure your firm can immediately initiate confidential sovereign and executive correspondence:
- **Mail Suite Provisioning:** Configuration of **Google Workspace** or **Microsoft 365** with custom vanity addresses (`haitham@econarc.com.sa`, `zytek@econarc.com.sa`, `inquiries@econarc.com.sa`).
- **Cryptographic Mail Hardening:**
  - **SPF:** Strict authorized sender policy (`v=spf1 ... -all`).
  - **DKIM:** 2048-bit cryptographic key signing to eliminate spoofing and guarantee message integrity.
  - **DMARC:** Enforcement policy (`p=quarantine` escalating to `p=reject`) with daily aggregate telemetry reports (`rua`) sent to your security mailbox.
- **DNS & Web Security:** DNSSEC signing, HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption, HSTS headers, and global edge CDN caching for sub-second page loads across Riyadh, London, Washington, and global policy hubs.
