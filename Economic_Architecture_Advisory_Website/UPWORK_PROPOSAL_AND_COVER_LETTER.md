### 1. Why Webflow is the Optimal CMS for Economic Architecture Advisory
1. **Frictionless Publishing for Advisory Staff:** Economists, researchers, and advisory leadership can draft, edit, and publish policy papers, working briefs, and announcements with visual on-page editing—requiring zero developer intervention or code deployment.
2. **Native Arabic RTL & Typographic Parity:** Native Webflow Localization applies `dir="rtl"` automatically for the Arabic locale, allowing us to implement exact typographic weighting using **Noto Sans Arabic** for Arabic paired with **Grenda** and **Satoshi** for Latin, with localized numeral formats and clean hreflang tags for regional SEO.
3. **Turnkey Sovereign Infrastructure:** Webflow eliminates database patching, server management, and security update vulnerabilities. The site is served across a Tier-1 global edge CDN (Fastly/AWS) with automated SSL, DDoS defense, and sub-second loading in Riyadh, London, Washington, and global policy hubs.
4. **Insights Repository & Large Document Architecture:** The Webflow CMS accommodates up to 20,000 items and 40 collections—far exceeding standard requirements. For research white papers and econometric datasets exceeding native 10 MB file limits, we integrate a dedicated high-speed cloud storage pipeline (Cloudflare R2 / AWS S3) linked via custom CMS fields, ensuring zero file-size restrictions for senior policy readers.
5. **Rapid 2–3 Week Delivery Velocity:** By utilizing Webflow’s semantic frontend engine, our engineering hours are invested directly into high-fidelity design implementation, brand geometry, and research repository UX rather than redundant backend maintenance.

### 3. Cryptographic Email Hardening & Sovereign Security (`econarc.com.sa`)

* **Custom Executive Routing:** Setup of Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailboxes (`haitham@econarc.com.sa`, `zytek@econarc.com.sa`, `inquiries@econarc.com.sa`).

* **DMARC Enforcement:** Strict policy (`p=quarantine` graduating to `p=reject`) with automated forensic aggregate reporting (`rua`) sent to a dedicated security mailbox.
