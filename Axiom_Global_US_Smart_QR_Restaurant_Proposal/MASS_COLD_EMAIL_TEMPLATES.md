## 📌 CRITICAL COLD EMAIL DELIVERABILITY RULES (Before Sending to 100+ People)

1. **NEVER use standard "BCC" for 50+ recipients in Gmail/Outlook:**
   - Email providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo) flag mass BCC emails as spam immediately.
   - **Instead, use a Mail Merge tool** that sends individual 1-to-1 emails automatically:
     - **Free/Affordable Tools:** **GMass** (adds mail merge directly inside Gmail), **Mailmeteor**, **Instantly.ai**, or **Apollo.io**.
     - These tools pull directly from your Excel sheet (`{{First_Name}}`, `{{Restaurant_Name}}`, `{{Business_Email}}`).
2. **Handling Attachments on Mass Outreach:**
   - Sending 1.4 MB PDF files + MP4 videos in cold initial emails will trigger spam filters.
   - **Best Practice:** Keep the first email lightweight (text-only or Google Drive link). Tell them: *"I’ve attached our 8-page proposal and 60-second video demo below (or I can send it over)"*. If your tool supports attachments, attach the PDF only, or share a clean viewing link.
