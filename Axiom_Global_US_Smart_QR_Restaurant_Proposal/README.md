# Axiom Global — Smart QR Restaurant Ordering System (US Commercial Proposal)

## Overview
This commercial proposal has been updated specifically for **United States restaurants, dining groups, and hotel food-and-beverage operations**. It presents a secure, app-free QR table ordering system designed to streamline dining room operations, eliminate server bottlenecks, and protect restaurants against unauthorized remote ordering.

## Core Updates in This US Edition

1. **Target Market Replaced (Saudi Arabia ➔ United States):**
   - Header changed to `UNITED STATES • RESTAURANT & HOSPITALITY TECHNOLOGY`.
   - Adapted for US dining dynamics, independent restaurant groups, and US compliance.
   - Payment processing updated to reflect US standards (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Stripe, Square, Toast, Clover).
   - Removed all regional Saudi references (such as mada payment cards, regional thobes/abayas, and Arabic lanterns).

2. **Pricing & Payment Structure Updated:**
   - **Total Proposed Investment:** **US$2,000** (updated from US$1,500).
   - **Two-Week Free Trial:** 14-day zero-risk operational pilot period.
   - **Option A — Full Payment:** **US$2,000** (one single payment after the 14-day trial).
   - **Option B — 3 Monthly Instalments:** **3 × US$667** (three monthly payments after the trial with 0% interest).

3. **Arabic Language Section Replaced (Feature 05):**
   - Removed the "05 Arabic + English smart menu" section.
   - Replaced with **"05 Smart Digital Menu & Dietary Filtering"**:
     - Instant dietary & allergen filters: Gluten-Free, Vegan, Vegetarian, Dairy-Free, and Nut Allergies.
     - High-definition dish photography and calorie/ingredient transparency.
     - Flexible item modifiers (meat temperature, dressing choices, side substitutions, protein add-ons).
     - Real-time 86-list / out-of-stock item toggling directly from staff devices to prevent kitchen delays.

4. **Visual Imagery Updated:**
   - Replaced all Middle Eastern photography with high-resolution, contemporary American restaurant interiors, lively dining rooms, modern kitchen display systems, and contactless digital payments.
