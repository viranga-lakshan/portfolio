2. Technical Architecture & Unifier Engineering Capabilities

Our software engineering pod approaches Oracle Primavera Unifier not merely as a configuration tool, but as the central nervous system of capital project governance, cost control, and schedule alignment:

Business Process Engineering

uDesigner & Workflow Automation

  • Workflow Routing Logic: Designing parallel, conditional, and consensus-based approval routes with escalation timers, auto-reassignment, and sub-processes.
  • Dynamic Data Elements (DDE): Configuring dynamic behavior on detail forms where field values dynamically dictate visibility, mandatory state, and read-only locks.
  • Data Picker & Auto-Population: Structuring data pickers linking project shells, vendor masters, cost codes, and master contracts with zero manual re-entry.
  • Reverse Auto-Population (RAP): Auto-updating parent contracts and master cost sheets upon final approval of commitment change orders.
Financial Governance

Cost Sheets, CBS & Cash Flow

  • Cost Breakdown Structures (CBS): Structuring project CBS codes aligned with general ledger accounting standards and EPC project control baselines.
  • Cost Sheet Column Formulae: Configuring Original Budget, Approved Changes, Revised Budget, Commitments, Actual Costs, Pending Changes, and Forecast at Completion (FAC).
  • Cash Flow Curves & Baselines: Generating distribution profiles (S-Curves, Linear, Bell) linking project schedules with planned capital expenditures.
  • Fund Allocation & Tracking: Monitoring funding source limits, public treasury allocations, and contractor retention drawdowns.
Enterprise Integration

Primavera P6 & ERP Synchronization Architecture

Primavera P6 Integration Engine:
  • Bi-directional linking of Unifier CBS codes with P6 Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and Activity IDs.
  • Schedule progress pull: Importing P6 early/late dates, percent complete, and milestone completions into Unifier cash flow.
  • Cost progress push: Exporting approved Unifier cost changes and actual spend to update P6 cost-loaded schedule baselines.
ERP / Financial Backbone Integration:
  • REST/SOAP integration adapters connecting Unifier to Oracle EBS, Cloud ERP, and SAP.
  • Automated Purchase Order & Subcontract commitment creation upon final Unifier approval.
  • AP Invoice clearance synchronization: Ingesting payment voucher numbers and actual disbursement dates from ERP into Unifier payment certificates.
Analytics & Intelligence
