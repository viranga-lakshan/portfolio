## Technical Specifications
- **Theme:** Lean Block-Theme Child (extending Twenty Twenty-Five) or bespoke block theme built from 9 Figma templates and component library.
- **Structured Fields:** Secure Custom Fields (SCF) with 4 structured tabs for the 70 legal use-case fields and repeatable rows.
- **Multilingual:** Polylang (Free) covering Macedonian (default), English, and German with synchronized page switching, translated slugs, and strict hreflang tags.
- **Contact & Lead Routing:** Forminator (Free) multi-step form routed by practice area to 5 lawyer inboxes + 15-minute call slot request with Honeypot / reCAPTCHA v3 anti-spam.
- **Divi Migration:** Automated PHP / WP-CLI regex parser stripping `[et_pb_*]` shortcode wrappers and converting ~200 articles into native Gutenberg blocks.
- **SEO & Canonical Strategy:** Full Screaming Frog crawl, 1:1 server-level 301 redirect map, category-to-area canonical links, XML sitemaps per language, and `LegalService` / `Person` / `Article` schema.
- **Timeline:** Launch on 17 December 2026 + 14 days of post-launch warranty.
- **Pricing:** Fixed price of $1,050 USD across 4 milestones (20% $210, 40% $420, 30% $315, 10% $105).
