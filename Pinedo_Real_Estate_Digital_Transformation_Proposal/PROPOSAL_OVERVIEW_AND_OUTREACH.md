### What the Proposed Platform Brings Together

Rather than managing properties, enquiries, and viewing schedules across fragmented tools, our proposal outlines a single, unified digital platform:

1. **Modern Property Discovery:** Fast, mobile-first faceted search (location, price, bedrooms, energy labels A-G) with interactive maps and high-resolution galleries.
2. **Centralized Property Management:** Streamlined back-office portfolio administration for residential, commercial, and rental listings with instant status toggles (*Beschikbaar*, *Onder Bod*, *Verkocht*).
3. **Automated Lead & Enquiry Routing:** Direct capture of web, WhatsApp, and email leads into an actionable CRM pipeline with automated agent dispatch.
4. **Interactive Viewing Calendar:** Real-time broker availability engine with two-way Google Calendar synchronization, automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders, and buffer time calculation.
5. **Business Intelligence & Analytics:** Visual performance dashboards tracking listing velocity, enquiry volume, and viewing conversion rates.
6. **Optional AI Property Assistant:** 24/7 natural-language conversational bot to qualify buyer budgets and arrange viewings after office hours.

### Technical & Engineering Foundation
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS (Server-Side Rendered for superior SEO).
- **Backend & APIs:** Node.js, Express.js RESTful APIs, PostgreSQL relational database with Prisma ORM.
- **Enterprise Integrations:** Google Maps API, WhatsApp Business API, and automated email triggers.
