SECTION 02 · DUFFEL API & TICKETING ARCHITECTURE
IATA ACCREDITATION MODEL
API SPECIFICATIONS

Why Duffel Flights API is the Optimal Choice

Historically, selling airline tickets required a travel agency to hold direct IATA / ARC accreditation, post a $20,000–$50,000 bank guarantee, and license complex legacy Global Distribution Systems (GDS like Sabre or Amadeus). Duffel modernizes this entirely by acting as an accredited IATA agency that handles ticketing on your behalf.

1. How Duffel Ticketing Operates

Duffel holds direct NDC (New Distribution Capability) and GDS agreements with over 300 airlines worldwide. When a customer books on Faris Travel USA, the order is routed to Duffel's API, which immediately issues the official electronic ticket under Duffel's authorized ticketing accreditation.

POST /air/orders Payload: selected_offers: ["off_001"], passengers: [details], payments: [balance] Response: status: "confirmed", booking_reference: "PNR123", documents: [e-tickets]
2. Financial Flow & Markup Capture

The customer pays via Stripe on Faris Travel USA. Faris Travel captures the full retail amount (Wholesale Airfare + Your Agency Markup). Duffel then issues the ticket using your pre-funded Duffel balance or automated card dispatch, leaving your net profit markup immediately in your bank.

Wholesale Airline Fare: $450.00 Faris Travel Markup (Configured): +$40.00 Customer Charged on Stripe: $490.00 Faris Travel Net Immediate Profit: $40.00
Duffel Flights API End-to-End Execution Pipeline
[Customer / Agency Agent on FarisTravelUSA.com] │ (Origin, Destination, Dates, Cabin, Pax Count) ▼ [Duffel Search: POST /air/offer_requests] │ (Aggregates live fares from 300+ NDC & GDS airlines) ▼ [Faris Travel Markup Middleware] │ (Injects Faris Travel margin: Flat $ or % markup) ▼ [Flight Results & Seat/Baggage Selection UI] │ (Customer enters passport/passenger information) ▼ [Secure Checkout: Stripe Payment Gateway] │ (3D Secure card charge: Wholesale Fare + Markup) ▼ [Duffel Order Creation: POST /air/orders] │ (Instant order confirmation & ticket generation) ▼ [E-Ticket Delivered: Airline PNR + 13-Digit E-Ticket PDF via Email]
Preserving Existing Website Infrastructure

Whether faristravelusa.com is built on WordPress, PHP, Webflow, or custom HTML, we embed the Duffel booking interface as a native, responsive module or dedicated booking page (e.g. faristravelusa.com/flights or seamlessly on the homepage search bar). YES — Our proposed solution fully supports live flight booking and automated electronic ticket issuance.
How Live E-Ticketing Works in Production

When an order is confirmed, Duffel automatically interfaces with the issuing airline's reservation system. The customer and Faris Travel receive:

1. Airline Booking Reference (PNR)

A genuine 6-character airline locator (e.g. W3X8KZ). The passenger can immediately take this PNR to the airline's official website (e.g. delta.com, emirates.com) to check in or view their itinerary.

2. Official 13-Digit E-Ticket Numbers

Duffel generates valid IATA electronic ticket numbers (e.g. 006-2384920192 for Delta or 176-9283748291 for Emirates). These are live, valid boarding documents.

Clear Explanation of Industry Limitations & Boundaries

To ensure 100% transparency, here are the real-world operational rules common to all airline aggregators:

Operation Capabilities Supported Known Limitations / Workflow Notes
Instant Ticketing Supported for all major scheduled carriers. Requires funding in your Duffel balance or automated card billing.
Low-Cost Carriers (LCCs) Supported (Ryanair, EasyJet, Spirit, etc.). Some LCCs issue direct confirmation codes instead of 13-digit IATA e-tickets.
Ticket Changes / Post-Booking Duffel API supports order change requests. Changes and refunds are subject to airline fare rules and fees.
Unpublished Consolidator Fares Available if Faris Travel has private airline contracts. Private negotiated fares require loading specific airline corporate discount codes.

How Duffel Funding Works for Ticketing

To issue tickets, airlines require payment at the moment of ticketing. Faris Travel USA maintains a small pre-funded balance in your Duffel dashboard (e.g. $1,000–$2,000 deposit via bank transfer) or connects a business card. When a customer pays $500 on your website, Stripe deposits $500 into your bank, while Duffel deducts the wholesale $460 from your balance.

Zero Hidden Subscriptions Guarantee

You will never be locked into expensive monthly GDS leases (which often cost $500–$1,500/month). The Duffel + Stripe model is strictly pay-as-you-go: if you sell zero tickets in a quiet week, your cost is $0. When you sell tickets, fees are deducted directly from the customer's payment.
